04 / Inside the broadcast

Every card is
part of the signal.

BOTCAST is a roguelike deckbuilder about building a radio show one decision at a time. Your lineup, its order, and the strange effects between cards decide how far your signal travels.

01 / SELECT
▤

Build your lineup

Draw a hand of broadcast cards: breaking news, music, conspiracy theories, late-night entertainment, and more. Choose the programs that fit your strategy.

02 / SEQUENCE
↝

Arrange your broadcasts in the right order. Some cards amplify the next one, others react to tags, repeat effects, or turn a risky show into a huge combo.
